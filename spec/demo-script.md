# Demo script — SiteGuard Stage I video (≤3 min, unlisted YouTube; Pintu records)

One take, fixed shots. Total budget 2:30.

- **0:00–0:20 — The problem.** Webcam or slides: "A worker without a hard hat is invisible until someone walks past with a clipboard." (15s)
- **0:20–0:45 — The concept.** One architecture slide: camera → vision-worker → policy engine → dashboard. "SiteGuard watches continuously and turns violations into auditable incidents." (25s)
- **0:45–1:45 — The live demo (screen recording).** Sample construction clip plays in the dashboard; bounding boxes track people; a person without a hard hat gets a "NO HARD HAT" label; after N frames the red alert banner fires and the incident log gains a timestamped row with snapshot. Narrate what the policy engine is doing. (60s — the money shot; do not rush it)
- **1:45–2:15 — Honesty beat.** "Stage I runs on a laptop with a pretrained detector and a rule-based policy loop — the simulation is labeled throughout. Stage II ports this to Thunderstorm silicon with LLM incident summaries." (30s — judges trust this)
- **2:15–2:30 — Close.** Repo link on screen. "SiteGuard: the safety officer that never blinks." (15s)

Recording notes: 1080p screen capture, dashboard at full-window, mic check before the take, keep the sample clip queued so the demo starts instantly.
